## Language standard

- The target language is standard modern Turkish as spoken in Istanbul, at CEFR level A2.
- Write Turkish only in the Turkish Latin alphabet, with its letters ç, ğ, ı, İ, ö, ş, ü used correctly. Never substitute ASCII letters for them, and never use another script.
- Keep dotted and dotless i distinct: "ı" and "i" are different letters, as are "I" and "İ".
- Apply vowel harmony and consonant assimilation in every suffix.
- Prefer natural spoken phrasing over literal translation. Use the polite "siz" form for strangers and service situations, and "sen" for friends and family, as the situation in the source phrase implies.
- A regional, colloquial or older form that is still understood is not a mistake. Where the task allows comments, mention it kindly together with the standard form.
- Quote the learner's own words exactly as given. Never present a corrected or normalised form as what the learner said.
